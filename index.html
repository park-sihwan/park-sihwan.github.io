Research themes

Five threads, one goal — systems that use less energy and emit less carbon.

01 / CRYOGENICS

Hydrogen Liquefaction

Conventional liquefiers operate far from their thermodynamic minimum work. I redesign the cycle itself — triple-pressure Claude cycles, bypass configurations, and flashing mixed-refrigerant precooling — to cut exergy losses and specific energy consumption.

Aspen HYSYSExergy analysis
02 / DATA-DRIVEN

Hybrid AI Modeling

Purely data-driven models break outside their training range. I combine first-principles models with machine learning to build surrogate and control frameworks that stay reliable and interpretable for real chemical plants.

Physics-informed MLSurrogate models
03 / CIRCULAR CARBON

Sustainable Methanol

Co-utilizing captured CO₂ and biomass can make green methanol cost-competitive. I design and evaluate production routes where feedstock synergy improves both economics and life-cycle emissions.

CO₂ utilizationLCA
04 / METHODOLOGY

Carbon-Techno-Economic Analysis

Cost and carbon are usually optimized separately. I develop CTEA — folding carbon-emission cost directly into techno-economic optimization — so a process can be tuned for economics and climate impact at once.

TEA + carbon costOptimization
05 / STORAGE

Energy Storage Systems

Which carrier stores large-scale renewable energy best? I compare hydrogen- and methanol-based storage on thermodynamic and economic footing, including cold-energy recovery from liquid hydrogen.

H₂ vs MeOHCold-energy recovery
